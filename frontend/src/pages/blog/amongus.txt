# the infinite among us pattern that stole 7 months of my life

= introduction
tiling (aka tessellation) is a topic that has absolutely enchanted me, and
if youre as big a fan of both math and art as i am, then maybe you'll be
enchanted too. we humans for all of history have covered our walls, our
streets, and our places of worship with tiles, and it is modern mathematics
that allows ameteurs like me to play around with and discover new, novel
tilings.

in fact, the tiling of today's focus, what i have dubbed *the among us tiling*,
seems to have been discovered independently by several ameteurs since the
popularization of among us in late 2020. 

the among us tiling has been a great vehicle for bettering my own mathematical
abilities. since, in general, i aim to solve the following question:

"for a pixel rendering of the among us tiling, how can you determine the
color of any given pixel (i, j)?"
